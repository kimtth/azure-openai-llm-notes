### **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**

- RAG integrates retrieval (searching) into LLM text generation, enabling models to access external information. [RAG vs Fine-Tuning](https://towardsdatascience.com/rag-vs-finetuning-which-is-the-best-tool-to-boost-your-llm-application-94654b1eaba7) [25 Aug 2023]
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks 📑💡: Meta's 2020 RAG framework introduced **RAG-Sequence** (retrieve once for the entire generation) and **RAG-Token** (retrieve per generated token), with **RAG-Sequence** becoming the industry standard due to its lower cost and simpler implementation. [22 May 2020] [Adding Your Own Data with RAG](https://towardsdatascience.com/add-your-own-data-to-an-llm-using-retrieval-augmented-generation-rag-b1958bf56a5a) [30 Sep 2023]
- [9 Effective Techniques To Boost Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) Systems](https://towardsdatascience.com/9-effective-techniques-to-boost-retrieval-augmented-generation-rag-systems-210ace375049) [🗄️](9-effective-rag-techniques.png): ReRank, Prompt Compression, Hypothetical Document Embedding (HyDE), Query Rewrite and Expansion, Enhance Data Quality, Optimize Index Structure, Add Metadata, Align Query with Documents, Mixed Retrieval (Hybrid Search) [2 Jan 2024]
- Advanced RAG Patterns 📑💡: Improve RAG through **data quality** (cleaning, annotation, updates), **embedding optimization** (domain fine-tuning and refresh), **retrieval optimization** (chunking, metadata, hybrid search, reranking, recursive retrieval, query routing, multi-vector retrieval, vector indexing, and **HyDE**), and **generation optimization** (query transformation, prompt engineering, function calling, and model fine-tuning) [RAG Pipeline Failures and Advanced Patterns (Part 1)](https://cloudatlas.me/why-do-rag-pipelines-fail-advanced-rag-patterns-part1-841faad8b3c2) / [Improving RAG Performance: Advanced Patterns (Part 2)](https://cloudatlas.me/how-to-improve-rag-peformance-advanced-rag-patterns-part2-0c84e2df66e6) [17 Oct 2023]
- [Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Survey on Agentic RAG📑](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09136) [15 Jan 2025]
- [Contextual Retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/news/contextual-retrieval): Contextual Retrieval enhances traditional RAG by using Contextual Embeddings and Contextual BM25 to maintain context during retrieval. [19 Sep 2024]
- [Enhancing Ultra High Resolution Remote Sensing Imagery Analysis with ImageRAG📑](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07688): Ultra High Resolution (UHR) remote sensing imagery, such as satellite imagery and medical imaging. [12 Nov 2024]
- From Simple to Advanced RAG (LlamaIndex) [Twitter Post](https://twitter.com/jerryjliu0/status/1711419232314065288) / 💡[Jerry Liu](https://aiconference.com/speakers/jerry-liu-2023/) [10 Oct 2023]

#### Cache

- Caching: A technique to store data that has been previously retrieved or computed, so that future requests for the same data can be served faster.
- To reduce latency, cost, and LLM requests by serving pre-computed or previously served responses.
- Strategies for caching: Caching can be based on item IDs, pairs of item IDs, constrained input, or pre-computation. Caching can also leverage embedding-based retrieval, approximate nearest neighbor search, and LLM-based evaluation. [Llm patterns](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/llm-patterns/#caching-to-reduce-latency-and-cost)
